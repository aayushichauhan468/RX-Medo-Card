Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2025

RX MEDO CARD, an initiative of RX Medical Trust, is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines in clear terms how we collect, use, and protect your data when you access our services.

Information We Collect

To deliver and continuously improve our healthcare discount services, RX MEDO CARD collects both personal and non‑personal information.

  • Personal Information: Includes your name, email address, phone number, residential address, payment details, and health‑related data necessary to provide our services.
  • Non‑Personal Information: Covers usage statistics, device information, cookies, and tracking data that help us improve functionality and user experience.
  • Third‑Party Information: Relevant data provided by partner hospitals, laboratories, or pharmacies to enable cashless treatment and associated offers.
How We Use Your Information

Facilitate membership registration and provide access to services. Schedule appointments and coordinate medicine delivery. Process secure payments and manage billing records. Share health‑related updates, tips, and relevant offers. Analyze usage patterns to enhance platform performance. Offer personalized senior care services within the Delhi NCR region. Ensure compliance with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

How We Share Your Information
Healthcare Partners: Shared only as necessary to provide eligible discounts and healthcare services. Service Providers: Disclosed to trusted vendors for payment processing or IT support, under strict confidentiality agreements. Legal Authorities: Released when required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or to protect public safety. With Consent: Shared for testimonials, promotional activities, or other purposes only with your explicit approval.

Note: RX MEDO CARD does not sell or rent your personal information under any circumstances.

Data Security
Encrypted transmission of sensitive information. Secure servers and controlled data access. Regular security audits and reviews.

While we take strong precautions, no system is 100% secure. In the case of a breach, we will notify users promptly.

Your Rights
