At RX Medical Trust, we put you first — our flexible cancellation policy is designed around your convenience, every time.

Order Cancellation

Section 01

We make cancellations simple — just reach out before your order is processed or shipped by our vendors or merchants. After fulfillment begins, cancellations may no longer be applicable, but we'll do our best to support you every step of the way.

Cancellation for Perishable Goods

Section 02

Perishable items such as flowers and eatables are non-cancellable due to their short shelf life. However, your satisfaction matters deeply to us — if the product delivered falls short of quality expectations, we'll gladly arrange a refund or replacement for you.

Damaged or Defective Items

Section 03

Damaged or defective item? We've got you covered. Report it to our Customer Service team and we'll work swiftly to resolve it. Every claim is verified by our merchant to ensure a fair and prompt solution — because quality is a promise we take seriously.

Product Not as Expected

Section 04

Received something that doesn't match what you ordered? Don't worry — we're here to make it right. Contact our Customer Service team after delivery, and we'll thoroughly review your case to ensure a fair and satisfying resolution.
