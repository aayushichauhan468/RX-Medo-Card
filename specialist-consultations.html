Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding specialist visits, hospital procedures, and membership savings.

What is the Specialist Consultation service through Rx Medo Card?

Specialist Consultation is an informational healthcare benefit that allows Rx Medo Card members to access senior medical specialists at empanelled multi-speciality hospitals. Members receive substantial outpatient (OPD) fee concessions simply by presenting their card at participating partner billing counters.

How much can members save on doctor consultations?

Members typically enjoy 20% to 50% concessions on outpatient specialist consultation charges across our network, depending on the hospital facility and department. Concessions apply directly to the consultation fee at registration.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a specialist?

No advance medical referral is required. If you hold an active Rx Medo Card, you can directly visit or schedule an appointment with your chosen specialist discipline (e.g., Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics) at any empanelled hospital.

Which hospitals can I visit for specialist consultations?

You can visit any hospital in our verified network, including MD City Hospital, Yashoda Hospitals (Sanjay Nagar, Nehru Nagar, Vasundhra), Medanta Hospital (Noida), Sarvodaya Hospital (Greater Noida & Faridabad), Felix Hospital, and others listed in our Hospitals Directory.

Can I consult doctors online or only in-person?

Both options are supported. In addition to in-person outpatient clinic visits at our partner hospitals, Rx Medo Card offers tele-consultation and digital e-consultations for non-emergency follow-ups, general advice, and routine health concerns.

How do I avail the discount at the hospital reception?

Simply show your physical Rx Medo Card or display your digital membership credentials with QR verification at the OPD registration desk when generating your consultation slip. The staff will verify your active membership and apply your member rate.

Can my family members use my card for their consultations?

If you have an Rx Medo Family Card or Family Secure plan, all covered family members (up to 6 members) are eligible for discounted specialist consultations across our partner hospital network.
