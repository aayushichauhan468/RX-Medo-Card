Package Anatomy

What Can a Hospital Package Include?

Hospital packages typically bundle multiple medical and facility components into structured healthcare plans. Learn how packages are organized across partner institutions.

Component 01

Room & Bed Accommodation

Hospital stay charges covering the assigned ward or room category (such as general ward, twin-sharing, or private room) based on package tier and hospital bed availability.

Component 02

Surgeon & Specialist Fees

Professional charges for the operating surgeon, assistant surgeons, anesthetist evaluation, and routine daily doctor consultation rounds during hospital stay.

Component 03

Operation Theatre (OT) & Equipment

Standard surgical suite utilization fees, specialized equipment usage, sterilization, and standard disposable surgical consumables utilized during the procedure.

Component 04

Pre-Procedure Diagnostics

Essential pre-operative blood work, baseline biochemistry tests, routine chest X-rays, and ECG screenings included as part of standard pre-surgical clearance bundles.

Component 05

Nursing & Paramedical Care

Around-the-clock patient nursing assistance, regular vital parameter monitoring, IV administration, and basic supportive hospital care during the recovery phase.

Component 06

Recovery & Discharge Summary

Immediate post-procedure recovery room monitoring, clinical discharge briefing, formal discharge documentation, and prescribed initial follow-up consultation guidance.

Important Transparency Note on Hospital Inclusions

Package inclusions, room categories, and concession structures are determined individually by each hospital and department depending on the clinical nature of the procedure, length of stay, and patient health condition. Rx Medo Card members receive direct member concessions (such as 10%–20% on hospital operations and short-term hospitalization, and 20%–50% on OPD/diagnostics). Patients are advised to consult the hospital billing desk or call our care helpline (+91 9999307517) to confirm specific package estimates prior to planned admission.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts regarding hospital packages, member savings, admission protocols, and package inclusions.

What is a Hospital Package through Rx Medo Card?

A hospital package refers to bundled inpatient or day-care treatment arrangements provided by our partner hospitals. Rx Medo Card acts as a discount healthcare program, entitling active members to verified concessions—such as 10%–20% on hospital operations and short-term hospitalization, and 20%–50% on diagnostics and OPD consultations—directly at participating billing desks.

How much do members save on hospital admissions and surgeries?

As documented across our membership plans (such as Rx Medo Card, SeniorShield, and Family Secure), members typically receive 10% to 20% concessions on hospital operations and short-term hospitalization, plus 20% to 50% on diagnostic tests and up to 20% on prescribed medicines.

Are all medical procedures and surgeries covered under hospital packages?

Specific procedures offered under package pricing vary by individual hospital facility and department. Common planned procedures include general surgery, laparoscopic procedures, orthopedics, day-care operations, and diagnostic packages. We advise members to contact the hospital reception or Rx Medo Card care desk to verify package availability for their specific medical condition.

How do I confirm the exact inclusions of a hospital package?

Package details (including room category, surgeon charges, pre-op tests, and length of stay) are specified in the hospital's official estimate. Present your active Rx Medo Card to the hospital billing or admission counter, or reach out to our healthcare helpline at +91 9999307517 before admission to confirm the applicable member rates.

Which hospitals can I visit for inpatient care and packages?

You can visit any participating hospital within our verified network, including MD City Hospital (Sector 122 Noida), Yashoda Hospitals (Sanjay Nagar, Nehru Nagar, Vasundhra), Medanta Hospital (Noida), Sarvodaya Hospital (Greater Noida, Sector 8 & 19 Faridabad), Felix Hospital (Sector 137 & Greater Noida West), Max Hospital (Vaishali), and SRS Hospital (Sector 70 Noida).

Is Rx Medo Card an insurance policy or hospital booking agency?

No. Rx Medo Card is a healthcare discount and savings program initiated by the Rx Medical Trust. It provides direct point-of-care member concessions across partner hospitals, diagnostic centers, and pharmacies. This page is purely informational and does not process medical admissions or hospital bookings online.

How do I avail the discount at the hospital admission desk?

Simply present your physical card or digital membership credentials with QR verification at the hospital's admission or TPA/billing desk during initial admission registration. The staff verifies your membership status and applies the agreed member rates directly on your bill.
