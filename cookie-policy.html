Information about how Rx Medo Card uses cookies, browser storage, and tracking technologies as described in the Rx Medo Card Privacy Policy.

1. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit www.rxmedocard.com. Alongside standard cookies, websites may use browser storage mechanisms (such as local storage and session storage) to keep pages responsive and remember interface states across visits.

We use cookies and related non-personal usage technologies to enhance your browsing experience, support smooth navigation, and maintain reliable functionality. As stated in our Privacy Policy, RX MEDO CARD does not sell or rent your personal information under any circumstances.

2. Categories of Technologies We Use

Different technologies serve distinct purposes when you browse our healthcare membership, hospital, diagnostic, and pharmacy pages:

Essential & Functional

Support core website navigation, responsive header and menu states, form submissions, and basic session continuity so key pages render and behave properly.

Preference Storage

Remember helpful display or interface choices during your visit so you do not have to repeat selections as you move between pages.

Performance & Diagnostics

Capture aggregate usage statistics and device compatibility signals that help us identify broken links, improve page speed, and refine user experience.

Third-Party Assets & CDNs

Pages may load fonts, icons, or UI libraries from trusted content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts and jsDelivr), which receive standard technical request metadata to deliver those assets.

3. Managing or Disabling Cookies

You stay in control of how cookies are handled on your device. Most modern web browsers allow you to inspect, block, or clear cookies at any time:

  • Browser Privacy Settings: Adjust cookie permissions or clear stored site data in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox settings.
  • Selective Blocking: You may block third-party cookies while keeping essential site functionality enabled.
  • Impact of Disabling: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some interactive features or saved preferences may not work correctly.
