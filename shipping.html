At RX Medical Trust, every order is delivered with care through our network of trusted courier partners ensuring your products reach you safely, securely, and on time.

Shipping for International Buyers

Section 01

All orders are shipped and delivered exclusively through registered international courier companies and International Speed Post — ensuring secure, reliable, and timely delivery right to your doorstep.

Shipping for Domestic Buyers

Section 02

All domestic orders are carefully dispatched through registered courier companies and Speed Post services — ensuring every delivery is secure, trackable, and right on time.

Shipping Timeline

Section 03

All orders are carefully processed and shipped within 22–35 days, or as per the delivery date confirmed at the time of your order. Please note that final delivery timelines are subject to the norms of our courier partners and postal services.

Delay in Delivery

Section 04

While RX Medical Trust ensures every order is handed over to our courier or postal partner within the committed timeframe, please note that delays caused by courier companies or postal authorities are beyond our control and outside our liability.

Delivery Address

Section 05

We deliver every order precisely to the address provided at checkout — because getting your package to the right place, safely and on time, is a responsibility we take seriously.

Delivery Confirmation

Section 06

The moment your order reaches you, a delivery confirmation will be dispatched instantly to your registered email ID — ensuring you're always informed and up to date.
